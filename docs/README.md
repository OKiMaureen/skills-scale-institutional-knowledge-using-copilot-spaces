# OctoAcme Project Management Documentation

Welcome to the OctoAcme Project Management documentation! This repository contains the comprehensive processes, workflows, and best practices used by OctoAcme to deliver high-quality projects through cross-functional collaboration and iterative delivery.

## Overview

OctoAcme's project management approach centers on cross-functional collaboration, iterative delivery, and a commitment to clear roles and accountability. Every initiative begins with a structured initiation phase: validating business needs, aligning stakeholders, and documenting scope and success metrics. Detailed planning follows, where work is broken into actionable increments, dependencies and risks are explicitly logged, and sprint milestones are mapped out to ensure transparency and alignment.

### Key Principles

- **Cross-functional collaboration**: Teams work together across disciplines to deliver value
- **Iterative delivery**: Small, testable increments ensure continuous progress and feedback
- **Clear roles and accountability**: Every team member understands their responsibilities
- **Transparent communication**: Regular updates, standups, and stakeholder reports keep everyone aligned
- **Continuous improvement**: Retrospectives and metrics drive ongoing process refinement

## Roles and Personas

Roles are crisply defined to facilitate focus and reduce ambiguity:

- **Project Manager**: Orchestrates delivery, manages schedules, and owns risk communication
- **Product Manager**: Defines outcomes, crafts and prioritizes the backlog, and measures impact
- **Developers**: Design, build, and validate features against clear acceptance criteria
- **Quality Assurance**: Specialists ensure quality standards are met through testing and validation
- **Stakeholders**: Participate in regular planning, reviews, and retrospectives

## Workflows and Processes

Workflows are built for consistency and repeatability. The team leverages the project board for transparent tracking through stages like backlog, in-progress, QA, and done. Pull Request workflows include mandatory issue linking, CI checks, code review, and clear acceptance criteria, ensuring new code meets quality and security standards.

Quality assurance blends automated testing pipelines—unit, integration, and smoke tests—with manual validation for critical feature acceptance, supported by regular demos and metric dashboards.

## Communication

Communication is a key pillar: daily standups, weekly syncs, regular stakeholder reports, and ad-hoc escalations keep momentum high and risks visible. Risks are documented in registers, escalated through clear paths, and addressed via cross-team updates. Retrospective meetings foster psychological safety, surfacing feedback and actionable improvements.
